Strip type

Wiring

The controller has five outputs, marked R, G, B, WW and CW on the board. If the strip's wires aren't in that order, the wiring check finds out and corrects for it, so you don't have to rewire. A single white is driven on both WW and CW until the check shows which one it's on.

Colour test

Shows one colour, to check the strip and its wiring.

Colour cycle

Slowly cycles through all colours.

Wi-Fi strength

Green is strong, red is weak. For finding a good spot for the controller.

A tool runs until you press Stop at the top of the page; then the strip goes back to normal. It isn't saved, so a restart also ends it.

What the door does

Opening or closing the door wakes the strip and restarts the idle timer. If the finished colour ends "when the door opens", it ends. That's all: the door never changes your settings.

Replaces the old "open and close quickly to toggle the lights" gesture. To turn the strip off, choose the Off mode above.
